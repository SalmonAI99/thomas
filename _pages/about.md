I’m Juntao Wu, a student and builder interested in **AI for coding and education**. I study computer engineering at the University of Michigan and mechanical engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My work focuses on coding agents and the harnesses that support them, especially evaluating how well agents diagnose and debug distributed systems. I also enjoy building tools for learning and exploring systems topics such as compilers, operating systems, and computer architecture.

My recent research project, **Cirdan**, investigates coding agents for distributed-systems debugging and led to **DistDebug-Bench**, accepted to the NeurIPS 2026 Evaluations and Datasets Track.
